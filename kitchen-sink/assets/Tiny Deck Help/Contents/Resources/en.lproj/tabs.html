The tabs

The tabs are ordered the way you reach for things while building an app. Switch with ⌘1 to ⌘0, or from the View menu.

Overview, App, Storage

Live system readouts and native dialogs; window, tray, menu, notification and update APIs; then files, SQLite, tiny.store, the per-app paths and the bundled resources.

Desktop, System

Shell open/reveal/trash, Spotlight, AppleScript, sharing, printing, the clipboard and screen capture. Battery, Wi-Fi, idle time, theme, sleep prevention and global hotkeys.

Media, GPU, WASM, Misc

Sounds and native audio filters; WebGL2 and WebGPU shaders; WebAssembly on both sides of the bridge; shell commands, backend fetch and FFI.

Every panel is searchable with ⌘F.
