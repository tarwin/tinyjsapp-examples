About
amp
a Winamp homage, drawn in CSS — no ripped skin bitmaps

Inspired by Nullsoft's Winamp and by Webamp, Jordan Eldredge's pixel-faithful Winamp reimplementation for the browser. It really whips the llama's ass.

Visualizers

Milkdrop — powered by butterchurn (MIT), the WebGL port of Ryan Geiss's MilkDrop 2.

Geiss HDR — derived from Geiss HDR © 2026 Ryan Geiss (Apache-2.0), the modern rewrite of the 1998 Geiss screensaver & Winamp plug-in.

Magnetosphere — amp's own homage to Robert Hodgin's Magnetosphere: particles riding a precessing magnetic dipole, hand-rolled WebGL, no deps.

Permutations — amp's own homage to John Whitney's Permutations (1968), made on a war-surplus analogue computer.

Full licenses ship in the repo: amp/src/geiss-hdr/
